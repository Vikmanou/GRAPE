# Example graphs

GRAPE ships four small assembly graphs for its "Load example" menu, gzipped
and otherwise unchanged.

## Acacia chloroplast (Unicycler) and Acacia mitochondrion (SPAdes)

`acacia-chloroplast-unicycler.gfa.gz` is `gfas-plastome/unicycler.gfa` and
`acacia-mitochondrion-spades.gfa.gz` is `gfas-mitome/spades_graph.gfa` from:

Anna Syme (2020). Supplementary files for manuscript: Acacia organelle genome
assembly. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5722622

Licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

## LPA kringle region (odgi)

`lpa-13-haplotypes.gfa.gz` is `test/LPA.gfa` from odgi,
https://github.com/pangenome/odgi (commit fb43f08).
